Offline PWA & 2G SMS Mesh Architecture

How AapdaSetu operates when cellular towers collapse

1. 2G Low-Bandwidth SMS Ingestion Protocol

In zero-internet flood zones, field workers send structured compact SMS to our gateway number:

ASETU SOS [SECTOR] [HAZARD_TYPE] [PEOPLE] [MED_URGENCY]

Example: ASETU SOS NAGAON FLOOD 150 TRAPPED

2. Explainable Rule Parser & Geocoding

The gateway extracts GPS or sector tokens, assigns standard weights, and injects the incident directly into the SDMA operator's dashboard.

3. Offline PWA Local Cache

Local state, emergency manuals, and priority matrices are cached via ServiceWorker and LocalStorage, operating 100% offline in air-gapped emergency tents.

Note: This is a simulated hackathon architecture walkthrough. Real production rollout connects to C-DOT Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) and Telecom Service Provider SMS gateways.

AapdaSetu Explainable AI (XAI) Methodology

Transparent, Deterministic Rescue Prioritization

In life-or-death disaster triage, opaque black-box AI models that hallucinate cannot be trusted. AapdaSetu uses an open-box, multi-criteria mathematical scoring model that produces transparent, auditable scores (0–100%):

Urgency Score = (0.35 × S) + (0.30 × P) + (0.20 × M) + (0.15 × R)
Factor Weight Parameters Evaluated
Disaster Severity (S) 35% Breach velocity, structural stability, rapid flood depth, landslide scale.
Population at Risk (P) 30% Logarithmic scaling of affected headcount (vulnerable elderly, children, trapped groups).
Medical Care Urgency (M) 20% Degree of entrapment, hypothermia risk, severed hospital connectivity.
Source Trust & Reliability (R) 15% NDRF / District Magistrate bulletins (100%), verified medics (85%), multi-citizen (65%), social media (40%).

Priority Tiers

  • CRITICAL (≥ 75%): Immediate amphibious/air rescue deployment within 45 minutes.
  • HIGH (50–74%): Perimeter stabilization, medical triage convoy dispatched within 2 hours.
  • MODERATE (25–49%): Relief supply distribution, water purification, 4-hour check-ins.

TinyFish Search & Fetch Architecture

Authoritative Ground-Truth Bulletin Cross-Verification

1. TinyFish Search API (State Bulletin Retrieval)

Dispatches real headless browser-rendered queries to retrieve fresh state gazetteers and SDMA press notes without scraping blockages:

GET https://api.search.tinyfish.ai?query=Kaziranga+NH37+flood&location=IN

2. TinyFish Fetch API (Clean Content & Media Hashes)

Renders authoritative pages, strips navigation noise/ads, and extracts keyframe hashes to detect recycled disaster imagery:

POST https://api.fetch.tinyfish.ai {"urls": ["https://asdma.assam.gov.in/..."], "format": "markdown"}

3. Secure Backend Proxy (Zero Client Credential Leak)

As mandated for production security, your TINYFISH_API_KEY is stored strictly in server environment variables (server-tinyfish.js), never in frontend JavaScript.
